performance, and protect against abuse, fraud, and security threats.

(b) Information you choose to provide. If you contact us directly (for
example, by email), we will receive the information you choose to
include in your communication.

(c) Future authentication and accounts. The Service does not currently
offer user accounts. If we introduce optional accounts in the future, we
may collect information such as an email address and basic profile
details. We will update this Policy before any such feature launches.

We do not currently collect precise geolocation data, payment
information, or other categories of personal information beyond what is
described above. Map and depth views you generate within the Service are
processed to render the requested visualization and are not used to
build a profile of you.
