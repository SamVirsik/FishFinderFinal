01 — Terrain

Read the seafloor like a topographic map.

Full-coverage coastal depth rendered from NOAA and USGS survey data. Color relief, hillshade, and slope let you read the bottom the way a contour map reads the land.

02 — Spotfinder

Find structure, then rank it.

Spotfinder scores the seafloor by slope, rugosity, and bathymetric position index, surfacing the drops, ledges, and hard bottom worth fishing or diving as a ranked list of detections.
