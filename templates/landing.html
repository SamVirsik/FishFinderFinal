Bathymetry

High-resolution terrain you can read.

Coastal depth rendered from survey-grade NOAA and USGS data. Color relief, texture relief, structure, and a fused spot score run client-side, so you can read the bottom the way a contour map reads the land.

  • Color relief with shaded hillshade overlay
  • Texture relief, curvature structure, and depth contours
  • Click anywhere for the exact charted depth
Spotfinder

Automatic structure detection.

Spotfinder scores a search area by slope, rugosity, and bathymetric position, then surfaces the ledges, drops, and hard bottom worth fishing or diving as a ranked list of detections you can jump to.

  • Draw a search box anywhere on the map
  • Ranked detections by structure type
  • Saved runs you can revisit and compare
3D terrain

Inspect the bottom in three dimensions.

Lift any patch of seafloor into a 3D view with depth labels and vertical exaggeration. Read walls, drop-offs, and transition zones from any angle to understand how structure actually sits on the bottom.

  • Tilt and orbit with adjustable exaggeration
  • Depth markers anchored to the surface
  • Same data as the 2D map — no re-fetch
Sources & tools

Compare sources, measure, and save.

Switch between bathymetric sources and satellite imagery to cross-check what you see. Measure distance across the bottom, and save the locations and runs that matter so they're ready next time.

  • Multiple NOAA sources plus satellite basemaps
  • Distance measurement across the seafloor
  • Saved locations and Spotfinder runs
