Data

Survey-grade depth, served raw.

FishFinder is built on public bathymetric and elevation data from NOAA and the USGS. The server fetches raw float32 depth grids and the browser does the rest — every color, hillshade, and slope is computed client-side from the real numbers.

Sources

Six public bathymetric sources from NOAA and the USGS, plus satellite imagery for cross-reference. Coverage varies by region — switch sources to compare what you see.
