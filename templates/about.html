About

Built to make the sea floor readable.

FishFinder turns public depth surveys into terrain you can actually read — the way a contour map reads the land — so anglers and divers can find structure before they leave the dock.

Who's behind it

Sam Virsik

Sam is a UC Berkeley student studying Data Science and Economics, with a background in financial quantitative research. He's also spent his life fishing and freediving — and got tired of trying to read structure from charts built for hydrographers. FishFinder is his attempt to bring the same analytical rigor he applies to markets to the ocean floor.

A note on safety

FishFinder is not for navigation. Depth values are derived from public survey data that may be incomplete or out of date — never rely on them for safe passage. Always boat and dive within your own limits and consult official charts. FishFinder is not affiliated with or endorsed by NOAA or the USGS.
